# Privacy policy — Mitzvot Plus

Updated: 2 October 2026.

Mitzvot Plus (the "app") is for daily mitzvot, zmanim, synagogues, and short learning. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact email: noambehor@gmail.com

## Account

An account is required. We store your email address and display name in Supabase Auth to run the account.

## Location

The app collects precise and approximate location only while the app is in use. Location is used to find synagogues, calculate zmanim, and check the minyan geofence. Coordinates and accuracy are stored on the user's minyan report. Location is not collected in the background.

## App activity

We store mitzvah preferences, mitzvah completions, quiz attempts, badges, and onboarding state.

## Notifications and files

Reminders are scheduled on the device. No push token is collected. Parasha podcast audio may be saved on the device. It is not uploaded as personal data.

## Processors

- Supabase: account and app data
- Google Maps: map display
- OpenStreetMap Overpass: synagogue search using the map area

Data is not sold. There are no ads and no analytics SDK.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the user deletes the account. Deletion removes the auth user and cascaded rows. Minyan reports the user filed are removed with the account. fulfilled_by_user_id on other reports is cleared so deletion is not blocked.

## Account deletion

Open Mitzvot Plus, sign in, open Profile, choose "מחיקת חשבון", and confirm.
