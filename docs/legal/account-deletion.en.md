# Account deletion — Mitzvot Plus

Contact email: noambehor@gmail.com

To delete the account and the data tied to it:

1. Open Mitzvot Plus.
2. Sign in.
3. Open Profile.
4. Choose "מחיקת חשבון".
5. Confirm with "מחק לצמיתות".

Deletion removes from Supabase the auth user, mitzvah progress, quizzes, badges, and minyan reports you filed. Location stored with a report is removed with that report. Location is not collected in the background.

If you cannot open the app, email a deletion request to noambehor@gmail.com from the address you used to register.
